# Awe

**Explore the world with clarity and curiosity.**

Awe helps you plan trips effortlessly with AI-generated itineraries while discovering daily travel inspiration designed to make you pause, learn, and feel connected to places across the globe.

Whether you’re planning your next journey or simply exploring from where you are, Awe blends thoughtful planning with lightweight discovery.

## What you can do with Awe

- ✈️ Create personalized travel itineraries in seconds  
- 📚 Save and revisit your trips anytime  
- 🌍 Discover daily travel facts and global stories  
- 🧭 Go on **Quests** — short, context-aware 1-hour adventures around you  
- 🌤 Get local weather context when permission is granted  
- 🗺 Explore destinations at your own pace
